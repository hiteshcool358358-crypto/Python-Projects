Salman Khan (born 27 December 1965) is an Indian actor, film producer, and television personality who works primarily in Hindi cinema. In a career spanning over three decades, his awards include two National Film Awards as a film producer, and two Filmfare Awards as an actor. He has been cited as one of the most popular and commercially successful actors of Indian cinema,[4] and was included in Forbes lists of the world's highest-paid celebrities in 2015 and 2018.
